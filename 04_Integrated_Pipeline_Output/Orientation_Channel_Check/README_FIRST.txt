CAPPIMU ORIENTATION CHANNEL SCAN
================================

This is a read-only inventory of knee_r(ex/ey/ez), accelerometer and gyroscope channels.
It does not transform data, normalize features, retrain a model, or modify raw files.

Do not assume the Euler fields are degrees or use them for a coordinate rotation until
their units, Euler order, axis convention and reference frame are verified in official
dataset/device documentation.
