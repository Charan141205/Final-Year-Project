Engineering interpretation:
  - Q8 (8 fractional bits, scale 256): 19/110,949 mismatches (0.0171%); zero on test.
  - Q10 (10 fractional bits, scale 1024): 3/110,949 mismatches; zero on validation and test.
  - Q12 (12 fractional bits, scale 4096): 2/110,949 mismatches; both are in training; zero on validation and test.
  - Q14 and Q16 further reduce mismatches but exceed signed int32 range under a single global scale.
  - Q12 requires 31 signed bits according to the dataset maximum, so signed int32 is sufficient for this dataset-wide range.
  - Q12 is the current practical uniform-scale candidate, subject to reproducing the same rounding, feature arithmetic and overflow behavior in C++.
